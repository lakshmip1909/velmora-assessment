
# VELMORA
## Commercial Growth Plan — Before Black Friday 2026

**Objective:** Increase completed purchases from existing traffic before investing in further customer acquisition.

### 1. Fix mobile checkout conversion

**Evidence:** Between April and September, mobile generated 25,097 sessions but only 109 orders (0.43% conversion), compared with 1.98% on desktop. Only 14.4% of mobile checkout starts resulted in orders.

**Action:** Audit payment failures, checkout forms, delivery costs and Instagram's in-app browser. Prioritise guest checkout, express wallets and transparent shipping information.

**Measurement:** Track checkout starts, payment failures and completed orders by device. Target mobile checkout completion of 20%, up from 14.4%, with 25% as a stretch goal.

### 2. Reduce fit and delivery uncertainty

**Evidence:** Mobile customers added products to their carts at a similar rate to desktop customers, but fewer progressed through checkout. Sizing and delivery concerns are hypotheses to test, not established causes.

**Action:** Show production and shipping estimates near product prices, alongside sizing and returns information. Provide an event-date checker and an AI-assisted shopping stylist that ranks only catalogue-verified products, with delivery safeguards.

**Measurement:** Track product-to-cart conversion, cart-to-checkout progression, delivery-checker usage and stylist-assisted conversion. Target mobile cart-to-checkout progression of 60%, up from approximately 53.6%.

### 3. Prioritise email and profitable acquisition

**Evidence:** Paid social produced £3,376 revenue against £2,700 advertising spend (1.25x ROAS). At a 55% gross margin, approximately 1.82x ROAS is needed to cover advertising spend before other costs. Email generated 52 orders from 1,744 sessions (2.98% conversion).

**Action:** Reduce inefficient cold paid-social spending. Prioritise email capture, occasion-based messaging and carefully timed Black Friday communications for 27 November 2026. Avoid blanket discounts that unnecessarily reduce margin.

**Measurement:** Track sign-up rates, email-driven orders, revenue per session, campaign gross profit and paid-social contribution after advertising costs.

### Implementation and constraints

**Sequence:** Checkout measurement and improvements first; fit and delivery confidence second; targeted email campaigns third.

Respect production capacity of approximately 20 made-to-order pieces per week. Test changes against a baseline and treat targets as goals, not guaranteed outcomes.

**Prototype limitations:** The website demonstrates discovery, AI-assisted catalogue ranking and delivery estimates, not a live checkout. The AI model runs in the browser, uses no API key and falls back to catalogue ranking if unavailable. Email registration is illustrative and does not store customer information.
