# VELMORA
## Commercial Growth Plan — Before Black Friday 2026

**Objective:** Increase completed purchases from existing traffic before investing in further customer acquisition.

### 1. Fix mobile checkout conversion

**Evidence:** Between April and September, mobile generated 25,097 sessions but only 109 orders (0.43% conversion), compared with 1.98% on desktop. Only 14.4% of mobile checkout starts resulted in completed orders.

**Action:** Audit the mobile checkout journey, focusing on payment failures, form complexity, delivery costs and Instagram's in-app browser. Prioritise guest checkout, express payment options and clear shipping information.

**Measurement:** Track checkout completion, payment errors and orders by device. Target an increase in mobile checkout completion from 14.4% to 20%, with 25% as a stretch goal.

### 2. Reduce delivery and fit uncertainty

**Evidence:** Mobile customers added products to their carts at a similar rate to desktop customers, but fewer progressed through checkout. The data does not establish why; uncertainty about sizing and delivery is a hypothesis to test.

**Action:** Display production and shipping estimates near product prices. Provide clear size options, returns information and an event-date delivery checker. Use catalogue-grounded product recommendations to help customers identify suitable pieces.

**Measurement:** Track product-to-cart conversion, mobile cart-to-checkout progression, delivery-checker usage and stylist-assisted conversion. Aim to improve mobile cart-to-checkout progression from approximately 53.6% to 60%.

### 3. Reallocate spend towards email and retention

**Evidence:** Paid social generated £3,376 in revenue against £2,700 advertising spend (1.25× ROAS). At a 55% gross margin, approximately 1.82× ROAS is needed to cover advertising spend before other costs. Email delivered 52 orders from 1,744 sessions, achieving 2.98% conversion.

**Action:** Reduce inefficient cold paid-social spending. Prioritise email sign-ups, occasion-based messaging and carefully timed communications ahead of Black Friday on 27 November 2026. Avoid blanket discounts that erode margins.

**Measurement:** Monitor email sign-up rates, email-driven orders, revenue per session, campaign gross profit and paid-social contribution after advertising costs.

### Implementation priorities and constraints

**Sequence:** Checkout measurement and fixes first; delivery and fit confidence improvements second; focused email campaigns third.

Monitor production capacity of approximately **20 made-to-order pieces per week** and avoid promotions that create unrealistic delivery expectations. Treat performance targets as testable goals, not guaranteed results.

**Prototype limitations:** The website demonstrates product discovery and delivery estimates rather than a live Shopify checkout. Its stylist uses transparent, catalogue-grounded rules, not a generative AI model. Email registration is illustrative and does not collect customer data.
