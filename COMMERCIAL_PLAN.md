
# VELMORA
## Commercial Growth Plan — Before Black Friday 2026

### Objective
Increase completed purchases from existing traffic before investing in more customer acquisition.

### 1. Fix mobile checkout conversion

**Evidence:** Across April–September, mobile generated
25,097 sessions but only 109 orders, a conversion rate
of 0.43%. Desktop converted at 1.98%. Only 14.4%
of mobile checkout starts became orders.

**Action:** Audit the mobile checkout journey, especially
payment methods, form complexity, delivery costs and
Instagram's in-app browser. Prioritise guest checkout,
express wallets and clear shipping information.

**Measurement:** Track checkout starts, payment failures,
checkout completion and completed orders by device.
Target an improvement in mobile checkout completion
from 14.4% to 20%, with 25% as a stretch goal.

### 2. Reduce delivery and fit uncertainty

**Evidence:** Mobile customers added products to their
carts at a similar rate to desktop customers, but fewer
progressed through checkout. The data does not establish
why; delivery timing and sizing uncertainty are hypotheses
to test.

**Action:** Show production and shipping estimates directly
under product prices. Add an event-date checker, clear
size options, returns information and catalogue-grounded
recommendations.

**Measurement:** Track product-to-cart rate,
cart-to-checkout rate, delivery-checker use and
conversion among users who interact with the stylist.
Aim to improve mobile cart-to-checkout progression
from approximately 53.6% to 60%.

### 3. Reallocate spend towards email and retention

**Evidence:** Paid social generated £3,376 revenue from
£2,700 advertising spend, a 1.25x ROAS. At a 55% gross
margin, approximately 1.82x ROAS is required to cover
advertising spend before other costs. Email generated
52 orders from 1,744 sessions, a 2.98% conversion rate.

**Action:** Pause or tightly limit inefficient cold paid
social campaigns. Prioritise email capture, occasion-based
messages and carefully timed Black Friday communications.
Avoid blanket discounts that unnecessarily reduce margin.

**Measurement:** Track email sign-up rate, email-driven
orders, revenue per session, campaign gross profit and
paid social contribution after advertising spend.

### Priorities and constraints

Implement checkout measurement first, then delivery
confidence improvements, followed by focused email
campaigns. Monitor production capacity of approximately
20 made-to-order pieces per week. Treat all targets as
hypotheses, not guaranteed outcomes.

The website is a concept demonstration, not a live
Shopify checkout. The stylist uses catalogue-grounded
rules and does not claim to be a generative AI model.
